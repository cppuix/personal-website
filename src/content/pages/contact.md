---
title: "Contact"
description: "How to share feedback, corrections, or suggestions for the site."
---
For feedback or corrections, the best place is the source repository or the platform where this site is hosted.

This page exists to keep communication simple and respectful. If you have a suggestion for the library, a translation update, or a project idea, it can be shared through the repo or the public contact channel.

The site avoids collecting personal data and is built with privacy as a first principle.
