title: "About"
description: "A short overview of the site, its purpose, and design values."
---
This site is a personal library and writing space built with privacy and simplicity in mind.

It brings together essays, project work, and reading resources in a calm, readable layout.

The site is designed to be lightweight and quiet: no external trackers, clean typography, and support for Arabic content.

New content is managed through the CMS so the site can stay up to date without changing the code.
