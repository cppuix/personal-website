<span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">Since camera apps make images without us pressing the capture button:
 opening the app and using it as a mirror is not appropriate; it is 
haram (impremissible).</span><div class="telegram-text"><p><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">If it is said:</span></p>
<blockquote>
<p><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">We did not take a picture, as it is not fixed and disappears when the app is closed.</span></p>
</blockquote>
<p><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">One could say to that:<br />
The imitation is still fulfilled. What you see on the screen is not a reflection, but automatic image-making.<br />
The image being permanent is not necessary for imitation to be fulfilled; creation itself is the condition for that.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">Based on this, using a phone camera as a mirror is not allowed, and it is a major sin.</span></p>
<h4 id="important-note"><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">Important Note</span></h4>
<p><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">If it seems strange to you that the app creates pictures without 
pressing the capture button, this indicates a lack of understanding of 
the nature of photography and how these devices work, and does not mean 
it is not considered photography.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">The advice is: Do not speak about what Allaah ruled, without knowledge. This matter concerns a major sin.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">Therefore, please consult experts before issuing a ruling on a purely scientific matter related to these modern devices.</span></p>
