<p dir="rtl" style="text-align: right;"><span style="font-family: Amiri;">&nbsp;بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم،</span><br /></p><p>Just like muscles need to be moved to stay in shape and good health, so does the mind, and the heart.<br /><br />A stale mind, one that is idle, doesn't react, doesn't initiate, doesn't seek, will suffer atrophy.<br /><br />Remember that Allāh commanded us repeatedly to reflect and think and take heed. If you stop doing that, you'll only harm yourself.<br /><br />A stale heart, one that is idle, doesn't react, doesn't initiate, doesn't seek, will suffer atrophy.<br /><br />Remember that Allāh commanded us repeatedly to do good, show mercy, repent, reckon our own selves before He—Exalted is He—does.<br /><br />A heart is sealed when it just...exists.<br /><br /></p><p dir="rtl" style="text-align: right;"><span style="font-family: Amiri;">قال تعالى: { لمن شاء منكم أن يتقدم أو يتأخر } [المدثر: 37]</span><br /></p><p>"To whoever wills among you to proceed or stay behind". Tr. Sahih International.<br /><br />Seeking good leads to more good.<br />Seeking ideas leads to more ideas.<br /><br />If you move, things move.<br /><br />Don't expect those beautiful water ripples from stillness.<br /><br />The fact that Allāh told us that we just have to seek means, has such a deep meaning, you won't understand unless you apply it. Even so, you'll only understand it to the extent of your application. It's too deep for any human consciousness.<br /><br />Move, Allāh yahdīk, move!</p>
