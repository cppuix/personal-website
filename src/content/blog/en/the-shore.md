<div dir="rtl" style="text-align: right;">بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم،<br /></div><div><br />Our scholars are our safe shore.<br /><br />We feel audacious and we swim far, look back to the shore and think: I can be far, I know how to swim!<br /><br />However, the sea is ruthless, and it has devoured countless excellent swimmers.<br /><br />The wise from the audacious folks swims far but keeps himself at a good distance, ready to circle back into safety. They can enjoy being adventurous, but still know and feel they're helpless and would always need to go back to the safe shore.<br /><br />The oblivious from the audacious swims further and further and further. The shore? Hah! That's for babies! I'm a big boi now!<br /><br />The veil of night begins to release its weight on the sky, and the breeze of the wind causes the skin the chills.<br /><br />The wise return to safety, into the warmth of the sand, and maybe some snacks!<br /><br />The oblivious finds himself locked, with no chains or locks. Locked by the darkness of sea, and darkness of night, and darkness of his own idiocy...until something drags him deep into the abyss...<br /><br />عياذا بالله.<br /><br />Stick to your scholars, الله يهديك.<br />Allāh commanded that.<br /><br />—Abū Sahl, ʿAbdurraḥmān bnu Mīhūb al-Qaddārī al-Jazāʾirī</div>
