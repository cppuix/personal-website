<p dir="rtl" style="text-align: right;"><span style="font-family: Amiri;">&nbsp;بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم،</span><br /></p><p>Allāh made this universe, and He, ʿazza wa jall, made our brains.<br /><br />Our brains are programmable, meaning, what you feed them, what you repeat, what you train them to seek…becomes how they function.<br /><br />So it follows that the best programming for the human mind is what Allāh commanded.<br /><br />Because the One who made the mind is the One who gave the instructions.<br /><br />The more a person aligns themselves with that guidance, the more correctly the mind operates: Clearer priorities, less confusion, less chasing illusions.<br /><br />The mind starts functioning the way it was meant to!<br /><br />Intelligence is not about appearing smart, or sounding modern, or saying smart things things, because that's just theater.<br /><br />Being smart serves no better purpose than seeking the truth, and seeking the truth is what actually makes you smarter.<br /><br />Why?<br />Because truth disciplines the mind.<br /><br />It forces honesty, humility; it forces you to drop nonsense even when your ego likes it!<br /><br />Falsehood does the opposite: it trains the brain to defend nonsense, justify contradictions, and that makes the cables tangled and messy.<br /><br />It's that simple.<br /><br />The more a person sincerely seeks the truth, the more their mind sharpens.<br /><br />Not everyone becomes a genius, but everyone moves closer to the best version of the intellect Allāh gave them.<br /><br />Truth improves the mind!<br /><br />An improved mind recognizes truth more easily.<br /><br />Rinse, and repeat.<br /><br />"But...akhi...life is not that simple..."<br /><br />I know I know...that's another story, called:<br /><br />The heart.</p>
