I suggest translating "سيد" as "chief".<br><br>Not "boss", not "head", not "president", none of that.<br><br>Because "chief" doesn't necessarily convey ruling. It is also used to mean status and position, which fits perfectly.<br>الحمد لله.<br><br>For example, if you want to say<br>الحسن والحسين سيدا شباب الجنة.<br><br>There is no leadership or ruling in al-Jannah. That ḥadīth means they are of the highest rank. "Chief" works here amazingly. الحمد لله.
