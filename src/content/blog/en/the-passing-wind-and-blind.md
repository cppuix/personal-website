<p>Even a passing wind has aim,<br />it moves with force, direction, and claim.<br /><br />Not every wind becomes a storm —<br />yet bends the branch and shifts the form.<br /><br />The dunes are carved by grains of sand,<br />not by the thunder's heavy hand.<br /><br />The stone is worn by what persists,<br />not sudden strikes from angry fists.<br /><br />The seed is carried and fields are grown,<br />by what you barely felt or known.<br /><br />The blind eye names it nothing still —<br />the valley fills against its will.</p>
