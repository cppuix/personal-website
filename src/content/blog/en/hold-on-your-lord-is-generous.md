<p dir="rtl" style="text-align: right;"><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">&nbsp;مهلًا، فربك كريم<br /></span></p><p><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">Hold on — your Lord is Generous.</span></p>
<p dir="rtl" style="text-align: right;"><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">قال الله تعالى: {قُلْ عَسَى أَنْ يَكُونَ قَرِيبًا} [الإسراء: 51].<br /></span></p><p><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">
Allaah, Exalted is He, said: { Say: Perhaps it will be soon } [Al-Israa': 51]</span></p>
<p dir="rtl" style="text-align: right;"><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">في هذه الآية تسلية للمؤمن المنتظر وفرج للأمل الكامن في صدره،<br /></span></p><p><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">This verse is soothing for believers who wait, and a relief for the hope hidden within their chests.</span></p>
<p dir="rtl" style="text-align: right;"><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">فهي تذكره أن وعد الله لا يطول على من صدق،<br /></span></p><p><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">
It reminds them that the promise of Allaah is never far for those who truly believe.</span></p>
<p dir="rtl" style="text-align: right;"><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">وأن ما يتمناه من خير قد يكون أقرب مما يظن،<br /></span></p><p><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">
And that the good they long for may be nearer than they imagine.</span></p>
<p dir="rtl" style="text-align: right;"><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">ولكن الحكيم سبحانه يؤخر لحكمة<br /></span></p><p><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">
Yet the All-Wise, Holy is He, delays for a reason full of wisdom,</span></p>
<p dir="rtl" style="text-align: right;"><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">ويعطي في الوقت الأنسب لعبده،<br /></span></p><p><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">
and grants at the most suitable time for His slaves.</span></p>
<p dir="rtl" style="text-align: right;"><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">فلتبقى القلوب معلقة بربها<br /></span></p><p><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">
So let hearts remain attached to their Lord,</span></p>
<p dir="rtl" style="text-align: right;"><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">واثقة أن التأخير ليس نسيانًا بل إعدادًا،<br /></span></p><p><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">
trusting that delay is not neglect, but preparation.</span></p>
<p dir="rtl" style="text-align: right;"><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">وأن ما كتب الله كائن في أوانه،<br /></span></p><p><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">
And that what Allaah has decreed will occur in its appointed time.</span></p>
<p dir="rtl" style="text-align: right;"><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">فلا تيأس إذا أبطأت الأمنية،<br /></span></p><p><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">
So do not despair when your wish seems slow to come.</span></p>
<p dir="rtl" style="text-align: right;"><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">فربك يقول: قل عسى أن يكون قريبًا،<br /></span></p><p><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">
For your Lord has said: <b>Say, perhaps it will be soon</b>.</span></p>
<p dir="rtl" style="text-align: right;"><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">فكن على يقين أنه قريب حقًا وإن لم تره بعد.<br /></span></p><p><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">So be certain that it truly is near, even if you have not seen it yet.</span></p>
<p dir="rtl" style="text-align: right;"><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">أبو المنذر الحوباني<br /></span></p><p><span style="font-family: IBM Plex Sans Arabic;">
Abu l-Munthir al-Hawbaanee.</span></p>
