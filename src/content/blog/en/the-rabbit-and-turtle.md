<p dir="rtl" style="text-align: right;"><span style="font-family: Amiri;">بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم،</span><span style="font-family: inherit;">&nbsp;</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">You ever notice how the <b>fastest</b> things you hear are usually the ones you <b>regret</b> trusting?</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">Clips and quotes, "<i>bro did you see this?!</i>".</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">It hits you <b>instantly</b>, it <b>feels</b> clear, it <b>feels</b> decisive; almost <strike>satisfying</strike>.</span></p><p>It tricks you with its looks and whispers: "what if you could reach the end without going through the process?".<span style="font-family: inherit;">&nbsp;</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">And then… a day later, a week later, a year later, or when you finally look into it properly…</span>it starts <b>falling</b> apart — <b>from your own mind</b> — because it was <b>never</b> solid to begin with.</p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">Details were missing, context was off, the conclusion was bigger than what the evidence could carry. Those quotes sprinkled around? <b>Cherry-picked</b> from a bigger system that <b>invalidates</b> the cherry-picking.</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">But at the moment…it felt solid.</span></p><h4 style="text-align: left;"><span style="font-family: inherit;">Contrast&nbsp;</span></h4><span style="font-family: inherit;">Now, some contrast to seal the idea: compare that to</span><span style="font-family: inherit;">&nbsp;anything you actually <b>trust</b>.</span><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">Maybe a teacher you respect, a&nbsp;principle you rely on, even your own understanding of something important, something you would resort to when things get real, when masks are off, and reality is staring at you waiting for your next move.</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">Did any of that come fast? <b>Nope</b>.</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">That stuff takes: <b>time</b>, revisiting, <b>more time</b>, <b>even more time</b>, mistakes, more mistakes, correction, more mistakes, correction again, bigger picture, out-of-the-box thinking, context awareness, implications awareness, consequences…do you see how strong the <b>contrast</b> is?</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">It is <b>slower</b>, <b>heavier</b>, <b>less exciting</b>.</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">But now? It doesn't shake easily.</span></p>This seems obvious, when you are shown the explanation and the steps, but:<p><span style="font-family: inherit;">Falsehood moves <b>fast</b> because it's <b>light</b>.</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">It doesn't carry much: no real context, no conditions,&nbsp;no fear of Allāh in how it's applied.</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">It's just a statement, pushed forward.<br /></span>Even more dangerous when it looks scholarly because of the <b>quotes</b>.<br data-end="1418" data-start="1415" />
Even more when it targets your <b>moral stance</b>.<br data-end="1467" data-start="1464" />
<b>Mental pressure</b> is easier to create than clarity.</p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">So it travels easily. From person to person. From post to post.</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">Like a rumor. No resistance.</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">Truth is the opposite: it's heavy.</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">Not in a complicated way, just… <b>loaded</b>.</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">It comes with:</span></p><ul><li><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">context</span></p></li><li><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">limits</span></p></li><li><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">"this applies here, not there"&nbsp;</span></p></li><li><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">sometimes even "<b>I don't know yet</b>"&nbsp;</span></p></li></ul><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">That weight <b>slows</b> it down.</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">You can't just throw it around. You have to <b><i>carry</i></b> it.</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">Speed feels like strength <b>at first</b>.</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">If someone speaks quickly, confidently, with <b>quotes</b> and <b>conclusions</b>… it <b>feels</b> like they know what they're doing.</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">But a lot of the time, that speed is just a sign that <b>nothing is holding them back</b>.</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">No depth, No hesitation, no <b>accountability</b>.</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">Just...just momentum.</span></p><span style="font-family: inherit;">Meanwhile, someone who pauses…doesn't rush to label, says "this needs to be looked at properly"; that can feel weak if you're not used to it.</span><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">But it's <b>not weakness</b>; it's <b>weight</b>.</span></p><span style="font-family: inherit;">Think of it like this:</span><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">* A rumor can cross a city in an hour.</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">* A reputation takes years to build.</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">But when the rumor fades, what's left?</span></p><span style="font-family: inherit;">So when you see things spreading quickly — strong claims, heavy labels, <b>urgent warnings</b> — don't just ask "is this true?", you're <b>not</b> equipped for that.</span><p>Instead, as<span style="font-family: inherit;">k:</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">Why was this <b>so easy to say</b>?</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">What wasn't <b>carried</b> with it?</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">What got <b>dropped</b> so it could move this <b>fast</b>?</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">Oh...yes, these are just more in-depth questions about the <b>same thing</b>.&nbsp;</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">So:</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">Yo</span><span style="font-family: inherit;">u don't have to resolve everything you hear.</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">You don't have to keep up with every wave.</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">Some things are supposed to be slow.</span></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">And the fact that they are slow…is exactly why they last.</span></p><p style="text-align: left;"><span></span></p><!--more-->&nbsp;<p></p><p style="text-align: left;"><a href="https://en-armalqaddaaree.blogspot.com/2026/04/how-to-follow-my-latest-updates-via-rss.html" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">How to follow this blog</a></p><p><span style="font-family: inherit;">&nbsp;</span></p>
