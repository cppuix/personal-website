</div><br /><div>Source: Advice of sheikh Yaasir al-Adani to audio makers:&nbsp;https://t.me/aboammaar/5313</div><div><br /></div><div>--------</div><div><br /></div><div>I'd really love if we stopped decorating people with labels, especially the living.</div><div>No matter how "big" someone seems.<br /><br />Just call them <i>shaykh</i> and move on. <br /><br />Saves you from exaggeration and from saying things that aren't true.<br /><br />Some don't even deserve the title <i>shaykh</i>, yet it's being used so much it barely means anything now.<br /><br />Dr. Fayṣal al-Wādiʿī still treats the word with weight and refuses to adopt it. More people like that would be nice. May Allāh bless him.<br /><br />When al-Albānī lowered himself and al-Wādiʿī said, "we're basically nothing at this point", what does that say about the rest of us?<br /><br />Yes, that was humility. But also honesty. They knew the level of the scholars before them and knew they were nowhere near them.<br /><br />Strange thing: the lower the level gets, the bigger the labels become.<br /><br />عجيب.</div>
